Chemical Reaction Engineering Kinetics Guide

1. Integral Method

Evaluates candidate orders $n \in [0, 3]$. Integrates $-r_A = -\frac{dC_A}{dt} = k C_A^n$ and performs linear regression on transformed $C_A$ vs $t$. Refines around the maximum $R^2$ to find fractional orders with high precision.

2. Differential Method

Computes continuous rate values $-r_A \approx -\frac{\Delta C_A}{\Delta t}$ using central differences. Fits $\ln(-r_A) = \ln(k) + n\ln(C_A)$ to deduce continuous reaction orders directly from the slope.

3. Reversible Reactions ($A \rightleftharpoons B$)

Accounts for equilibrium limitation: $-r_A = k_1 C_A - k_2 C_B$. Linearizes via $\ln\left[\frac{C_A - C_{Ae}}{C_{A0}-C_{Ae}}\right] = -(k_1+k_2)t$ to recover forward $k_1$, reverse $k_2$, and equilibrium constant $K_{eq}$.

4. Autocatalytic Reactions ($A + B \to 2B$)

Product $B$ catalyzes its own formation: $-r_A = k C_A C_B$. Evaluates the linear relationship $-r_A$ vs $C_A \cdot C_B$ passing through the origin.
