Select Kinetic Model & Method

Choose how the rate law will be determined from your data
Integral Method nth-Order Scan
$-r_A = -\frac{dC_A}{dt} = k C_A^n$

Scans candidate orders $n \in [0, 3]$, transforms $C_A(t)$, evaluates linear regression, and refines to fractional order.

Differential Method Numerical Derivative
$\ln(-r_A) = \ln(k) + n\ln(C_A)$

Numerically differentiates $C_A$ vs $t$ and extracts continuous reaction order directly from the log-log slope.

Irreversible Reaction A → Products
$-r_A = k C_A^n$

One-way conversion model testing integrated rate forms for zero, first, second, and fractional orders.

Reversible Reaction A ⇔ B
$-r_A = k_1 C_A - k_2 C_B$

Accounts for chemical equilibrium: determines forward $k_1$, reverse $k_2$, and equilibrium constant $K_{eq}$.

Autocatalytic Reaction A + B → 2B
$-r_A = k C_A C_B$

Models self-accelerating reactions where product $B$ catalyzes reactant $A$ consumption.
