-r_A = k C_A^n. Tests n = 0, 0.25, 0.5 … 3 against C_A vs t (n=0), ln(C_A) vs t (n=1), or C_A^(1−n) vs t (n≠1), then refines around the best fit to a fractional order.
ln(-r_A) = ln k + n·ln(C_A). Differentiates C_A vs t numerically, then finds a continuous (fractional-capable) order directly from the slope of the log-log plot.
A + B → 2B, −r_A = k C_A C_B. Requires a measured B concentration alongside A at each time point.
A ⇌ B. Requires an estimate of the equilibrium concentration C_Ae (or the app will approximate it from your lowest observed concentration).
-r_A = k C_A^n. Same transparent order-testing engine as the Integral Method, for a strictly one-way A → products reaction.
